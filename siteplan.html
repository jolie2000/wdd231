Kampala Coffee Routes

A site plan for a friendly guide to Uganda's coffee culture and places to enjoy a cup in Kampala.

Site name

Kampala Coffee Routes — “Kampala” makes the guide's home base clear, while “Coffee Routes” suggests both discovering local cafés and learning how Ugandan coffee travels from origin to cup.

Possible domain: kampalacoffeeroutes.com (availability not checked).

Site purpose

Kampala Coffee Routes will help residents and visitors explore Uganda's coffee culture. The site will introduce coffee origins and brewing traditions, let people browse a curated directory of at least fifteen cafés and roasters, and help them choose a place by location, roast or visitor amenities. A short form will let readers save a visit note or suggest a place for future review.

Audience scenarios

  • Where can I find locally roasted Ugandan coffee near central Kampala?
  • Which cafés have Wi-Fi and a comfortable place to work?
  • What is the difference between coffee from Uganda's major growing regions?

The plan uses warm coffee and earth tones to connect the guide to its subject. The same palette will appear in the project site.

Typography

Display and section headings — Lora

A good cup begins with a good story.

Lora is a readable serif for the site name, page titles, and section headings. It adds a warm editorial character.

Body and interface text — DM Sans

Find a nearby café, compare details, and plan your next coffee stop.

DM Sans will be used for paragraphs, navigation, labels, forms, and directory details for clear reading at small sizes.

Home page wireframes

The assignment requires original black-and-white sketches for a small viewport and a wider viewport. Add photographs or scans of your own sketches below before submission.

Small viewport sketch: Draw a stacked header with brand and menu control, a short introductory hero and browse button, a featured coffee place, a compact filter/search area, then vertically stacked coffee cards and footer.

Wide viewport sketch: Draw the brand and horizontal navigation across the top, a two-column introductory hero, then a filter/sidebar beside a three-column directory grid, followed by a full-width footer.

Your sketches: Insert the two hand-drawn sketches here as images, each with descriptive alternative text.

Planned project structure

  • Home: introduction, featured places, and links into the guide.
  • Coffee places: a searchable directory populated from a local JSON file using Fetch, with at least fifteen entries and at least four details per entry.
  • Origins and brewing: an approachable overview of Uganda's coffee regions and brewing traditions.
  • Interactions: filters, a details dialog, a saved-favorites preference in local storage, and a form confirmation page.
  • JavaScript: ES modules, asynchronous fetch with try/catch error handling, array methods, template literals, and event listeners.
