A routine that fits real life

Keep practice small and steady.

Pick a short session, set one goal and give yourself time to repeat. Consistency matters more than a perfect performance.

Which instrument feels right?

Choose the one you want to come back to.

You can learn either instrument as a beginner. Think about the sounds you enjoy, where you will practice and what kind of music inspires you.

♬

Strings · portable

Guitar

  • Easy to carry and play with other people.
  • Learn a few chord shapes to accompany songs.
  • Fingertips need time to adjust to the strings.

A good fit if you picture yourself strumming songs.

▥

Keys · visual

Piano or keyboard

  • Notes are laid out clearly from low to high.
  • Play melody and chords at the same time.
  • A keyboard can be quieter and easier to store.

A good fit if you like seeing how notes connect.

A little focus goes a long way

Set a practice timer.

Choose a session length, then spend it on one clear goal: a chord change, a scale or a short melody.

A beginner-friendly session

Try this 15-minute routine.

  1. 03 min
    Warm up

    Play a few notes slowly and relax your hands.

  2. 05 min
    Practice one new skill

    Work on one chord, one scale or one hand position.

  3. 05 min
    Repeat a small pattern

    Connect two chords or play a short phrase evenly.

  4. 02 min
    Notice your progress

    Write down one thing that felt easier today.
